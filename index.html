Privacy Policy for EasyExplorer

Last updated: September 4, 2026

EasyExplorer ("the app") is a file manager for Android, developed by Deepak Ranjan Biswas. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Summary

EasyExplorer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no analytics, no personalised advertising, no user accounts, and no network access of any kind. Every feature works entirely on your device.

What the app accesses

EasyExplorer requests broad storage access ("All files access") because browsing, searching, and managing files across your device's entire storage — internal memory and any SD card or USB drive — is the app's core purpose. This access is used only to:

  • List, open, and preview files and folders you browse to
  • Perform actions you explicitly request: copy, cut, paste, move, rename, delete, create, and search
  • Share a file with another app when you choose to, via Android's standard share sheet

No file content, filename, folder structure, or usage data is ever read by anyone other than you, through the app's own on-screen interface. The app has no server, and none of this information leaves your device.

Data collection

EasyExplorer collects no personal information, does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and contains no advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind.

The app stores a small number of preferences locally on your device only (such as your preferred view mode, sort order, and whether you've dismissed certain in-app notices). This information never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Sharing files with other apps

When you choose to share or open a file, EasyExplorer hands that specific file to the app you select (e.g. Gmail, Google Drive, a messaging app) via Android's standard sharing mechanism. EasyExplorer does not see or control what that other app subsequently does with the file — that's governed by the other app's own privacy policy.

Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions, each used only for its stated purpose:

  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — to browse and manage files across your device's storage, the app's core function.
  • Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — the equivalent, classic permission used automatically on older Android versions.

Children's privacy

EasyExplorer is a general-purpose utility not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information at all.
